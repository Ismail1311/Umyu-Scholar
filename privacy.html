1. Introduction

UMYU Scholar ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform (the "Service").

By using UMYU Scholar, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We are dedicated to being transparent about our data practices and to providing you with control over your personal information.

🔑 Key Principle: We believe that your data belongs to you. We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our services, and we never sell your personal data to third parties.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of UMYU Scholar, including students, staff, lecturers, and visitors. Please read this policy carefully to understand our views and practices regarding your personal data and how we will treat it.

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, please contact us using the information provided in the Contact Us section below.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our Service, including:

2.1 Personal Information You Provide to Us

  • Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your full name, email address, and password (encrypted). We also store your account creation date and profile settings.
  • Profile Information: You may optionally provide additional information such as your profile picture, department, academic year, and research interests.
  • Uploaded Content: When you upload papers, software, or other content to the platform, we collect the content itself, metadata (title, authors, keywords, abstract), and the associated files (PDFs, ZIP archives).
  • Communication Data: When you contact us through our contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and message content.
  • Research Data: Information about your research interests, department, and academic discipline that you choose to share.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you interact with our Service, including pages viewed, time spent on pages, search queries, and navigation paths.
  • Device Information: We collect information about the device you use to access our Service, including IP address, browser type, operating system, device type, and screen resolution.
  • Interaction Data: We track interactions with content, including likes, bookmarks, downloads, and views.
  • Referral Information: We collect information about how you arrived at our site, including referral URLs and search terms.
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: We use cookies to enhance your experience and collect information about your browsing behavior.

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Cloudinary: When you upload files, Cloudinary processes and stores them. We collect the URLs and metadata provided by Cloudinary.
  • Firebase/Authentication: We use Firebase for authentication and database services, which may collect standard authentication data.

2.4 Sensitive Information

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, health data, or biometric data. We encourage you not to share such information through our Service.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

3.1 Service Provision

  • To create and manage your account
  • To enable you to upload, access, and download papers and software
  • To process your interactions with content (likes, bookmarks, downloads)
  • To provide search and filtering functionality
  • To display personalized content and recommendations

3.2 Service Improvement

  • To analyze usage patterns and improve the user experience
  • To identify and fix technical issues and bugs
  • To develop new features and functionalities
  • To optimize search results and content discovery

3.3 Communication

  • To respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • To send you important updates about the Service (security alerts, policy changes)
  • To notify you about new features and improvements
  • To send verification and password reset emails

3.4 Security and Compliance

  • To protect the security and integrity of our Service
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • To comply with legal obligations and regulatory requirements
  • To enforce our Terms of Service

3.5 Research and Analytics

  • To conduct internal research and analysis
  • To understand user behavior and preferences
  • To measure the impact and reach of academic research

📌 Important: We never use your personal information for targeted advertising or marketing purposes. Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the UMYU Scholar platform.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on the following legal bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: Processing is necessary for the performance of our contract with you (i.e., providing the UMYU Scholar service).
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests in operating, improving, and securing our Service.
  • Consent: Where you have given explicit consent for specific processing activities (e.g., email communications).
  • Legal Compliance: Processing is necessary for compliance with legal obligations and regulatory requirements.

⚖️ Your Choice: You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time. However, withdrawing consent may affect your ability to use certain features of the Service.

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

5.1 Service Providers

  • Cloudinary: We use Cloudinary to store and manage uploaded files (PDFs and software archives). Files are stored securely in Cloudinary's infrastructure.
  • Firebase: We use Firebase (a Google service) for authentication, database, and analytics. Firebase handles your authentication data and stores your account information.

5.2 Publicly Shared Information

  • Papers and Content: Your uploaded papers, software, and associated metadata (title, authors, abstract, keywords) are publicly accessible on the platform.
  • Public Profile: Your name, department, and uploaded content are visible to other users.

5.3 Legal Requirements

  • We may disclose your information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental regulation.
  • We may disclose information to protect the rights, property, or safety of UMYU Scholar, our users, or others.
  • We may disclose information in connection with an investigation of fraud, security breaches, or illegal activities.

5.4 Business Transfers

  • If UMYU Scholar is involved in a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, your personal information may be transferred. We will notify you before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

🔒 Privacy Promise: We never sell your personal data to third parties for any purpose. Your information is used exclusively to provide and improve the UMYU Scholar service.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

6.1 Security Measures

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS protocols (HTTPS).
  • Authentication: We use Firebase Authentication with secure password hashing and email verification.
  • Access Control: Access to your personal information is restricted to authorized personnel who need it to perform their duties.
  • Secure Storage: Your data is stored in secure Firebase and Cloudinary infrastructure with industry-standard security measures.
  • Regular Monitoring: We monitor our systems for security vulnerabilities and potential threats.

6.2 User Responsibilities

  • You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.
  • You should use a strong, unique password for your UMYU Scholar account.
  • You should not share your account credentials with others.
  • You should log out of your account when using shared or public devices.

⚠️ Important: While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

7.1 Retention Periods

  • Account Information: Retained for as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time.
  • Uploaded Content: Retained as long as you keep your papers on the platform. You may delete your uploads at any time.
  • Interaction Data: Retained for analytics and service improvement purposes for a reasonable period.
  • Inquiries: Contact form submissions are retained for support and record-keeping purposes.

7.2 Data Deletion

  • User Deletion: You may request account deletion by contacting us. Upon verification, we will delete your account and associated data.
  • Paper Deletion: You may delete your uploaded papers at any time through your profile page.
  • Data Exports: You may request a copy of your data by contacting us.

After deletion, some residual data may remain in our backups for a limited period before being permanently erased. Publicly shared content that has been downloaded by other users may continue to exist outside our control.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on UMYU Scholar.

8.1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and understand how you use our Service. They are essential for the functioning of many website features.

8.2 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the operation of our Service, including session management and authentication.
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and preferences.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our Service and improve the user experience.

8.3 Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. However, please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our Service.

🍪 Cookie Notice: We use only essential and functional cookies. We do not use third-party tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

9. Your Rights

You have certain rights regarding your personal information. We are committed to facilitating the exercise of these rights.

9.1 Summary of Rights

  • Access: You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Rectification: You have the right to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: You have the right to request the deletion of your personal information.
  • Restriction: You have the right to restrict the processing of your personal information.
  • Data Portability: You have the right to receive your personal information in a structured, commonly used format.
  • Objection: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal information.
  • Withdraw Consent: You have the right to withdraw consent where we rely on consent for processing.

9.2 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of your rights, please contact us using the details provided in the Contact Us section below. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

  • To access your data: Request Data Export
  • To delete your account: Request Account Deletion
  • To correct your information: Update your Profile Settings

9.3 Complaints

If you believe we have not handled your personal information in accordance with applicable data protection laws, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

10. Children's Privacy

UMYU Scholar is intended for university-level students, staff, and researchers. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child without parental consent, we will take steps to remove that information.

11. International Data Transfers

UMYU Scholar is based in Nigeria. Our Service providers (Firebase and Cloudinary) may process data in various locations around the world.

  • Firebase: Data is processed in Google's data centers located in various regions globally.
  • Cloudinary: Data is processed in Cloudinary's data centers located in various regions globally.

By using our Service, you consent to the transfer of your data to these locations. We ensure that appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your data in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

🌍 Data Location: While our primary operations are in Nigeria, we use global cloud services to ensure reliable performance. Your data may be stored on servers located outside Nigeria.

12. GDPR Compliance

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

12.1 Data Protection Officer

We have appointed a Data Protection Officer to oversee our data protection practices. You can contact our DPO at umyuscholar@gmail.com.

12.2 GDPR Rights

In addition to the rights listed in Section 9, EEA residents have the right to:

  • Right to be Informed: You have the right to be informed about how we collect and use your personal data.
  • Right of Access: You have the right to access your personal data and receive a copy.
  • Right to Rectification: You have the right to correct inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Erasure: You have the right to have your personal data deleted ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • Right to Data Portability: You have the right to receive your personal data in a portable format.
  • Right to Object: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data.
  • Rights Related to Automated Decision-Making: You have the right not to be subject to automated decision-making.

12.3 Lawful Basis for Processing

For EEA residents, we rely on the following lawful bases for processing your personal data:

  • Contract: Processing is necessary for the performance of our contract with you.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing is necessary for compliance with our legal obligations.
  • Legitimate Interests: Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests, which are not overridden by your interests or fundamental rights.
  • Consent: Processing is based on your explicit consent.
