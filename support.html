Frequently Asked Questions

Only UMYU students and staff with verified @umyu.edu.ng (staff) or @students.umyu.edu.ng (student) email addresses can upload papers. Guests can browse and download papers freely.

  1. Sign in with your UMYU email address.
  2. Go to the Upload page from the sidebar.
  3. Fill in all required fields (title, authors, department, year, keywords, abstract, contact info).
  4. Upload your PDF file (max 10MB).
  5. Optionally upload a ZIP/RAR/7z file for software (max 50MB).
  6. Click "Upload Paper".
  7. Your paper will appear on the homepage immediately after successful upload.
  • PDF files (required) – Maximum size: 10MB
  • ZIP, RAR, 7z files (optional, for software projects) – Maximum size: 50MB

Only these formats are allowed. Make sure your files are within the size limits.

If your upload is not appearing, please ensure:

  • Your email is verified (check your inbox).
  • You are using a valid @umyu.edu.ng or @students.umyu.edu.ng email.
  • You filled in all required fields correctly.
  • The PDF file is under 10MB and is in the correct format.
  • If all else fails, contact us for assistance.
